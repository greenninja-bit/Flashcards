    Describe the four main processes in the water cycle.

    1) Evaporation -> the sun makes water evaporate from the land and sea, turning it into water vapour
    2) Transpiration -> water evaporates out of the stomata of leaves
    3) Condensation -> water vapour cools and condenses to form clouds
    4) Precipitation -> water falls from the clouds and is returned to the land and sea

    Explain why the populations of the predators and prey often change in cycles

    The population of any species is usually limited by the amount of food available
    If the population of the prey increases, then so will the number of predators
    If the population of predators increases, then the number of prey will decrease, and therefore, so will the number of predators
