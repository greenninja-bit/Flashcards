Describe the four main processes in the water cycle.

1) Evaporation -> the sun makes water evaporate from the land and sea, turning it into water vapour
2) Transpiration -> water evaporates out of the stomata of leaves
3) Condensation -> water vapour cools and condenses to form clouds
4) Precipitation -> water falls from the clouds and is returned to the land and sea


What happens to the waste products and dead organisms in ecosystems?

They are broken down by decomposers


Give three factors that affect the rate of decomposition and explain how they affect it

1) Oxygen -> many decomposers need oxygen for aerobic respiration
2) Temperature -> most decomposers work best in warm conditions - decomposers contain enzymes, too high they denature too low they freeze
3) Water Content -> Decomposers need water to survive, in moist conditions rate increases BUT waterlogged soils don't contatin much oxygen so BADDD D:< (rate decreases)


What is meant by the terms 'population', 'community' and 'ecosystem'

Population -> All the organisms of one species in a habitat
Community -> All the organisms (different species)
Ecosystem -> A community of organisms along with all the non-living (abiotic) conditions


Give four resources that plants need in order to survive and reproduce. Do the same for animals.

List four abiotic factors and give examples of how they might affect communities in ecosystems. Do the same for 2 biotic factors.

Biotic factors -> Availability of Food, Number of Predators


Explain why the populations of the predators and prey often change in cycles

The population of any species is usually limited by the amount of food available
If the population of the prey increases, then so will the number of predators
If the population of predators increases, then the number of prey will decrease, and therefore, so will the number of predators
